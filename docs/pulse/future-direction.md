# Future Direction

**[Subscrypts Pulse](https://pulse.subscrypts.com)** already provides real-time subscription monitoring, push notifications, and multi-wallet support. Looking ahead, the focus is on deeper insights, richer notifications, and tighter integration with the broader Subscrypts ecosystem.

---

## 1. Enhanced Analytics & Insights

Planned dashboard improvements include:

- **Payment history charts** — Visual timeline of past payments and upcoming charges
- **Spending summaries** — Monthly or weekly breakdowns of subscription costs
- **Trend indicators** — Track changes in your subscription portfolio over time

These features will help subscribers make informed decisions about their subscriptions and budget planning.

---

## 2. Richer Notification Types

Future notification enhancements include:

- **Renewal reminders** — Configurable advance warnings before auto-renewal
- **Price change alerts** — Notifications when USD-denominated plan prices shift significantly due to token price movement
- **Payment failure notifications** — Alerts when an auto-renewal attempt fails due to insufficient balance

---

## 3. Merchant-Side Monitoring

While Pulse is currently focused on the subscriber experience, planned merchant features include:

- **Plan performance overview** — Track active subscriber counts per plan
- **Payment event feed** — Real-time feed of incoming subscription payments
- **Revenue indicators** — High-level revenue tracking for merchants

---

## 4. Ecosystem Integration

Deeper integration with the Subscrypts ecosystem:

- **Direct links to management actions** — One-tap navigation from Pulse to the [Subscrypts dApp](https://app.subscrypts.com) for renewing, stopping, or modifying subscriptions
- **Cross-platform status** — See which platforms (Discord, Telegram) your subscription gates access to
- **Shared notification preferences** — Coordinate notification settings across Pulse and other ecosystem tools

---

## 5. Platform Improvements

- **Improved offline support** — Better caching for full offline access to subscription data
- **Widget support** — Home screen widgets for quick balance and status checks (where platform APIs allow)
- **Accessibility improvements** — Enhanced screen reader support and accessibility features

Any such improvements will maintain Pulse's core principles: **read-only**, **custodian-free**, **privacy-first**, and **no app store required**.
